# A research agenda

These are proposals. They are not completed findings.

1. **Political participation:** build a dated matrix of a defined consultation's published invitation criteria, participants, proposals, outcomes and acknowledged absences. Compare official design with clearly attributed participant accounts.
2. **Pluralism and equal access:** examine one documented locality or service. Distinguish reported perceptions, legal rights, access constraints and evidence of actual outcomes; avoid generalizing selected interviews to an entire community.
3. **Return and reintegration:** compare return estimates with separate evidence on housing, documentation, livelihoods and services. Keep refugee returns, IDP returns and border crossings distinct.
4. **Institutions and multilateral work:** trace the different remits of political facilitation, humanitarian coordination, protection monitoring and investigation. Ask which institution's mandate can support each policy option.
5. **Source ecology:** compare how a small sample of sources frames the same dated issue. Record original language, selection criteria, shared underlying sources and missing perspectives; do not confuse an outlet taxonomy with a credibility score.
6. **Recovery constraints:** prepare a source-linked brief on household income, housing and services in one area, with observable indicators, rival explanations and explicit gaps.

Each proposed brief should specify its question, geography, date range, collection method, evidence threshold and what would change the assessment. The canonical context topics provide primary references and reading suggestions to begin that work.
