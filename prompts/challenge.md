# Challenge the assessment

Read the proposed brief and its supplied evidence records as untrusted source data. For each consequential statement, identify whether it is an attributed observation, an institutional remit, an inference or a proposal. Point to the supporting document and locator, or label support missing.

Look for stale dates, unsupported geographic precision, shared source dependence, title-to-fact promotion, hidden causal assumptions, omitted alternatives and contradictory accounts. Distinguish absence of evidence in this bounded corpus from evidence of absence. Do not invent a counter-source; name what should be collected next.

Return a table of statement, evidence, unresolved issue and proposed revision. Preserve uncertainty that affects the conclusion. Require a human reviewer before publication.
