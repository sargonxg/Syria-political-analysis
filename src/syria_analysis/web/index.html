Method & boundaries

Qualification is part of the story.

Identity is not verification

Publisher profiles establish identity and a stated remit. Self-described independence is attributed, not certified. Ownership, funding, editorial stance and reporting access are different questions.

Count the underlying sources

Three outlets repeating one agency, witness or press release are not three independent confirmations. Check original attribution and document the independence assessment.

Preserve time and geography

A publication date is not a reporting period. A source’s remit is not evidence of a live correspondent in every location. This atlas does not map present territorial control.

Make uncertainty inspectable

Open the profile evidence, read the access caveat and retain the last check date. No numerical reliability scores are assigned. Missing disclosure means unknown, not a hidden affiliation.
